GGIDataSync

An internal business integration tool operated by GGI to connect authorized GGI workflows with QuickBooks Online through Intuit APIs.

Internal use only · Not a public SaaS product
View Privacy Policy

What it does

GGIDataSync supports GGI's internal accounting workflow by creating invoices in bulk through the QuickBooks Online API.

Data accessed

The application reads authorized QuickBooks Online customer and product/item information needed for its workflows.

Data retention

GGIDataSync is designed not to retain QuickBooks Online customer or product data after processing, subject to necessary technical or security logging.

Legal & Privacy

GGIDataSync is intended for authorized GGI personnel and is not offered to the general public. For details about the application's use and data handling practices, please review the End-User License Agreement and Privacy Policy.
