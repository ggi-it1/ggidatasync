GGIDataSync Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GGIDataSync, an internal business application operated by GGI ("GGI," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information accessed from QuickBooks Online ("QBO") through Intuit's APIs.

GGIDataSync is an internal-use application and is not offered to the general public.

1. Information We Access

When authorized to connect to a GGI QuickBooks Online company, GGIDataSync may access information necessary to perform its internal accounting functions, including:

  • Customer information;
  • Product and item information;
  • Information required to create and submit invoices; and
  • Other QuickBooks Online information that is necessary to support the application's authorized functions.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through QuickBooks Online is used solely for GGI's internal business and accounting operations, including:

  • Selecting and identifying customers;
  • Selecting products or items for invoices;
  • Preparing invoice data;
  • Submitting invoices to QuickBooks Online through the Intuit API; and
  • Supporting internal reconciliation and operational processes.

GGI does not use QuickBooks Online data for advertising, sale, or unrelated commercial purposes.

3. Data Retention

GGIDataSync is designed not to retain QuickBooks Online customer or product data after the data has been processed for its intended purpose.

QuickBooks Online data may be temporarily present in application memory or transient processing systems while an API request is being processed.

Separate technical, security, or operational logs may exist where reasonably necessary to maintain, troubleshoot, monitor, and secure the application. Such logs are not intended to serve as a separate database of QuickBooks Online customer or product information.

4. Sharing of Information

GGI does not sell QuickBooks Online data.

GGI does not intentionally disclose QuickBooks Online customer, product, or invoice information to third parties except where necessary to:

  • Operate and secure the application;
  • Use Intuit's QuickBooks Online services and APIs;
  • Comply with applicable law or legal requirements; or
  • Protect the rights, security, and property of GGI, its users, or its systems.

5. QuickBooks Online and Intuit

GGIDataSync connects to QuickBooks Online using Intuit's APIs and authorization mechanisms.

QuickBooks Online and associated Intuit services are provided by Intuit and are governed by Intuit's applicable terms, privacy policies, and security practices.

GGI does not control the privacy or security practices of Intuit. Users should review Intuit's applicable policies for information about Intuit's handling of data.

6. Security

GGI uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information accessed by GGIDataSync against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Access to GGIDataSync and connected QuickBooks Online data is limited to authorized personnel and systems.

7. Access Revocation

Authorization to access a QuickBooks Online company may be revoked through the applicable Intuit or QuickBooks Online authorization controls.

Once access is revoked, GGIDataSync will no longer be able to access that QuickBooks Online company through the revoked authorization.

8. Children's Privacy

GGIDataSync is an internal business application and is not directed to children. GGI does not knowingly collect personal information from children through GGIDataSync.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

GGI may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, business practices, security practices, or applicable requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
