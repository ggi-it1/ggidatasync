1. Purpose of GGIDataSync

GGIDataSync is an internal-use software application designed to integrate with QuickBooks Online ("QBO") through Intuit's APIs.

The application is used by authorized GGI personnel to:

  • Read customer and product information from GGI's QuickBooks Online company;
  • Create and submit invoices to QuickBooks Online in bulk through the QuickBooks Online API; and
  • Support internal accounting and operational workflows.

GGIDataSync is intended solely for authorized internal use by GGI and is not offered as a public or commercial application to third parties.

Users must not attempt to access QuickBooks Online companies, data, or accounts for which they are not authorized.
